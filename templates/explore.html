Explore Our AI Platform

CGI.R Auto Intelligence uses predictive models and real-time database filtering to simplify your car search.

Our Technology

  • Trained on a diverse dataset of cars using Linear Regression to achieve high R-squared scores.
  • Database Filtering: Uses direct SQL queries to a live MySQL database for fast, accurate recommendations based on your budget and preferences.
  • Input Guardrails: Built-in checks prevent impossible values (e.g., extremely low mileage on used cars, historically inaccurate model years) from skewing results.

How to Use

Choose your path on the home page:

  • **Predict Price:** Input specific car details (Year, Mileage, Engine Size) to get a single, scientifically predicted market value.
  • **Car Recommendation:** Use the multi-step form to define your ideal car (Budget, Fuel Type, Brand) and receive a list of matching listings from the database.
